Cancellation Policy

Rules and guidance for cancelling flights, hotels, cabs, and travel protection bookings.

Cancellation Requests

Customers can request cancellation by contacting TravelYaraa support with booking ID, registered email, phone number, service type, and cancellation reason. Cancellation is not complete until it is confirmed by the system or support team.

Cancellation charges depend on the booking type, time of cancellation, provider rules, fare conditions, hotel rules, cab rules, insurer conditions, and payment status.

Some bookings may become non-cancellable close to travel date or after provider confirmation. Customers should check cancellation rules carefully before booking.

Flights And Hotels

For flights, cancellation rules may depend on fare type, airline policy, route, travel date, ticket status, and time remaining before departure. Customers may also contact the airline directly for urgent cancellation, airport-related support, or operational updates.

For hotels, cancellation rules may depend on property policy, check-in date, room type, offer condition, season, and no-show rules. Some hotels may charge one night, partial amount, or full amount if cancellation is late.

TravelYaraa support can help guide cancellation requests, but airline or hotel rules may decide the final refund amount and eligibility.

Cabs And Travel Protection

For cab bookings, cancellation may depend on reporting time, vehicle assignment, driver dispatch, route, provider rule, and last-minute cancellation conditions.

For travel protection or insurance-related services, cancellation and refund may depend on policy status, coverage start date, claim status, and insurer terms.

Customers should request cancellation as early as possible to reduce charges where possible.

For booking support, correction guidance, cancellation assistance, refund status, or document delivery help, contact TravelYaraa support with your booking ID and registered contact details.
