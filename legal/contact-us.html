Contact TravelYaraa

For help with a booking, payment, cancellation or refund, contact TravelYaraa support by email or phone. Including your booking details helps us find your booking quickly.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

  2. What to include
  3. What you can do in My Bookings

  5. When to contact the airline or hotel

Contact details

  • Email: support@travelyaraa.com
  • Phone: +91 72310 60177

If your question is about a specific booking, email is best because you can include your booking details and screenshots.

What to include

  • Your Booking ID
  • The lead traveller's or guest's name
  • The email address or mobile number used for the booking
  • The travel or stay date
  • A short description of the problem
  • For payment problems: the payment reference and the amount, if you have them

Never share your full card number, CVV, card PIN, UPI PIN, one-time code (OTP) or passwords with anyone, including people who say they are from TravelYaraa.

What you can do in My Bookings

Sign in with the same account you used for the booking, then open My Bookings. Depending on the booking, you can:

  • See the booking status and booking details
  • Download your flight e-ticket, hotel voucher or invoice once the booking is confirmed
  • Resend the booking email
  • Check cancellation charges and request a cancellation
  • Send a change request

Payment taken but no confirmation

Please do not pay again. Some bookings show a pending status while we confirm them with the airline or hotel. Check the status in My Bookings. If the booking is not confirmed, or the status is unclear, contact us with your Booking ID and payment reference. If a booking cannot be confirmed after payment, see the Refund Policy.

When to contact the airline or hotel

Some things are decided by the airline or hotel on the day of travel. You can contact them directly about:

  • Airline: online check-in, airport reporting time, terminal, boarding, baggage at the airport and flight status on the day
  • Hotel: check-in and check-out times, early check-in or late check-out, accepted ID, deposits and charges payable at the property

We can still help with anything related to your TravelYaraa booking.
