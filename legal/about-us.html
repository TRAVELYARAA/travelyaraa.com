About TravelYaraa

TravelYaraa.com is an online travel booking website. You can search and book flights and hotels, pay securely online, and manage your bookings from your account.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What you can book

Flights. One-way, return and multi-city searches, with fare rules, baggage information and optional extras such as extra baggage, meals or seats where the airline offers them.

Hotels. Hotel stays with room options, meal plans, cancellation conditions and hotel rules shown before you pay.

Cab search appears on the home page, but cab booking is not available yet. We will only show a booking option for a service once it can actually be booked.

How booking works

  1. Search and choose a flight or a room.
  2. Review the details: dates, fare or room rules, cancellation conditions and the price summary.
  3. Enter traveller or guest details exactly as they appear on government ID.
  4. Pay the final amount shown at checkout.
  5. We place the booking with the airline or hotel. The booking is confirmed only when your booking page shows it as confirmed.

After booking, you can sign in and open My Bookings to view your booking, download documents, check cancellation charges or send a request.

Our role and the airline's or hotel's role

TravelYaraa arranges your booking: we show available options and their rules, collect your details and payment, place the booking, send your booking documents, pass on cancellation and change requests, and help you when something goes wrong.

The airline operates the flight and the hotel provides the stay. Their own rules decide things like check-in, baggage, boarding, accepted ID, deposits and house rules. We show the rules we receive from them during booking, and you should read them before paying.

Travel guides

We also publish free travel guides, such as Goa, Rajasthan and hotel booking tips. They are general planning help, not offers or listings.
