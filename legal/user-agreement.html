User Agreement

This agreement covers how you may use the TravelYaraa website and your TravelYaraa account. Bookings are covered by our Terms & Conditions and by the airline or hotel rules shown during booking.

Using the website

By using TravelYaraa.com you agree to use it lawfully and only for genuine travel searches and bookings for yourself or for people who have allowed you to book for them.

Your account

You can sign in with your mobile number using a one-time code, or with Google. Signing in lets you see your bookings in My Bookings and, if you choose, reuse saved traveller details.

  • Keep access to your phone, email and Google account secure.
  • Do not share one-time codes with anyone.
  • Tell us at support@travelyaraa.com if you think someone else has used your account.

Giving accurate details

Enter traveller and guest names exactly as they appear on the government ID or passport that will be used for travel, and give a working email address and mobile number. Airlines and hotels can refuse service, and changes may not be possible or may cost extra, if details are wrong.

Booking for other people

If you book for other travellers, you confirm that they have agreed to you sharing their details with us for the booking. Please share the booking details, rules and any updates with them, because we will send messages to the contact details given on the booking.

Saved traveller details

When you book a flight while signed in, you can choose to save traveller details for future bookings. The option is shown on the booking form and you can untick it before continuing. To ask about removing saved details, contact support@travelyaraa.com. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

What is not allowed

  • Making bookings you do not intend to use, or bookings in someone else's name without their permission
  • Using a card, bank account or UPI ID you are not authorised to use
  • Trying to get around security checks, prices or payment steps
  • Copying or collecting content or prices from the site using automated tools
  • Interfering with the website or other users

Where we reasonably suspect fraud or misuse, we may stop a booking attempt, review a booking or restrict an account. If this affects a paid booking, we will contact you, and any refund will be handled as described in the Refund Policy.

Website content

Text, design and logos on this site belong to TravelYaraa unless stated otherwise. Photos in our travel guides belong to their creators and are credited in each guide. Flight and hotel information comes from airlines, hotels and our booking partners.

Changes to this agreement

We may update this agreement when our services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
