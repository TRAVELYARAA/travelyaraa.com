Flights, hotels, cabs, and travel protection services are operated by their respective service providers. Their rules may apply to baggage, check-in, cancellation, refund, schedule change, room allocation, vehicle allocation, claims, and service limitations.

Customers should contact the airline, hotel, cab provider, insurer, or relevant service provider for operational matters such as boarding, terminal rules, room rules, local taxes, vehicle reporting, or claim documents.

TravelYaraa may assist with booking support and communication guidance, but the final decision on many service matters may remain with the relevant service provider.
