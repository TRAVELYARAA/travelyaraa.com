Cookie Policy

This page explains what the TravelYaraa website saves in your browser and which third-party services may set their own cookies.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

On this page

  1. In short
  2. What we save in your browser
  3. Third-party services
  4. Managing storage and cookies
  5. Changes

In short

  • TravelYaraa's own pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies.
  • We save some information in your browser's storage so that sign-in, search and booking work.
  • Sign-in and payment services provided by third parties may set their own cookies when you use them.

What we save in your browser

We use your browser's local storage and session storage. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab. Local storage stays until you sign out or clear it.

PurposeExamples
Keep you signed inSign-in status and basic profile, such as your name
Remember your searchLast search details and search results context
Keep a booking in progressThe selected flight or room, traveller details you have typed, and optional extras
Show payment resultsThe status of a recent payment, so the right result page appears
Remember preferencesYour language choice

Third-party services

  • Sign-in: signing in with Google, or with your mobile number and a security check, uses services that may set cookies or use similar technology to keep sign-in secure.
  • Payment: our payment partner's secure checkout may set cookies needed for payment and fraud checks.
  • Page resources: some pages load styles and icons from content delivery networks. Like any website request, these receive your IP address and browser details.

These providers handle cookies under their own policies.

Managing storage and cookies

You can sign out, and you can clear cookies and site data for travelyaraa.com in your browser settings. If you block or clear this data, you may be signed out, lose a search or booking in progress, or be unable to complete payment.

Changes

If we start using analytics or other cookies, we will update this page first. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
